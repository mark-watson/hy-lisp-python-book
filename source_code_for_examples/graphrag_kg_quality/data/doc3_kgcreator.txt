KGCreator is a knowledge graph extraction utility created by Mark Watson.
KGCreator processes plain text documents with the spaCy natural language processing library to identify named entities.
After entity extraction, KGCreator maps relationships to the schema.org vocabulary and resolves entity strings to DBpedia URIs.
The resulting RDF statements can be loaded into rdflib or a custom SPARQL datastore.
